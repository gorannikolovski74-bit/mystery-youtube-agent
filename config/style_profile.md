# Style Profile — Unsolved Mysteries Channel

> Faceless YouTube channel. Stickman / 2D animation + AI narration.
> Target: monetization via AdSense (1,000 subscribers + 4,000 watch hours).
> This file is the single source of truth for tone, structure and visuals.
> It is loaded verbatim into the Script Agent's prompt — keep it concise.

---

## CHANNEL

- **Theme:** Unsolved mysteries, cold cases, unexplained events, lost history.
- **Format:** Faceless. Minimal stickman animation over a dark background.
- **Inspiration:** "What Did Ancient Humans Do at Night?" — simple visuals,
  strong narrative, curiosity-driven.
- **Target audience:** 18–35, global, English-speaking.

---

## SCRIPT

- **Tone:** Mysterious, authoritative, calm. Never sensationalist or clickbaity
  in the body. Let the facts create the tension.
- **Length:** 750–850 words (~6–7 minutes of voiceover).
- **Structure:**
  1. **Hook** (first 15 seconds) — an intriguing question or a shocking fact.
  2. **Background** — set the scene: who, where, when.
  3. **Evidence** — the concrete facts, the strange details.
  4. **Theories** — the competing explanations, none confirmed.
  5. **Cliffhanger** — leave the question open.
- **Voiceover-friendly:** short sentences, flowing prose, no bullet points,
  no headings, no stage directions. Plain text only.
- **Ending (verbatim):** `What do YOU think happened? Let us know in the comments.`
- **Hard rules:**
  - Never name real, living people.
  - Every fact must come from the provided source summary. No invention.
  - No graphic, gory, or exploitative detail.

---

## VISUALS

- **Background:** dark navy `#1a1a2e` or black `#0d0d0d`.
- **Stickman:** white, simple movements. One idea per scene.
- **Accent color:** gold `#FFD700` for emphasis / key moments.
- **Subtitles:** white, Arial Bold, lower third of the screen.
- **Resolution:** 1920×1080, H.264.

---

## THUMBNAIL

- Dark background (`#0d0d0d`).
- Max **5 words**, large bold white text.
- Gold question mark or mysterious symbol (`#FFD700`).
- No faces (faceless channel).
- Resolution: 1280×720.
- **Title formula:** `UNSOLVED: [Short question]?`
  e.g. `UNSOLVED: What Killed 9 Hikers?`
